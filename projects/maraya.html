OVERVIEW

About the Project

Maraya Al-Ula is a 3D graphics project inspired by one of Saudi Arabia's most iconic landmarks and the surrounding landscapes of Al-Ula.

The project recreates Maraya within a desert environment surrounded by rock formations, open skies, natural elements, and atmospheric lighting.

SCENE

Building the Environment

The scene combines the architecture of Maraya with the natural environment of Al-Ula. The mirrored building reflects the surrounding mountains, sky, lighting, and elements of the scene.

Decorative lights were placed around the area, while a pathway-like surface leads toward the building to create a more immersive composition.
