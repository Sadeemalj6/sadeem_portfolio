    Little Mermaid

    Developed in 48 Hours — Global Game Jam

    Developed with Team Infinity Creators

    OVERVIEW

    About the Game

    Little Mermaid is a 2D game developed during Global Game Jam at King Abdulaziz University within a 48-hour development challenge.

    The game follows a mermaid traveling inside a bubble while collecting pearls and avoiding dangerous creatures throughout the level.

    GAMEPLAY

    How It Works

    Players control the mermaid as she moves through the underwater environment, collecting pearls while avoiding the creatures along the way.

    Pearl Collection

    Collect pearls while progressing through the underwater environment.

    Obstacle Avoidance

    Avoid dangerous creatures and obstacles throughout the level.

    2D Gameplay

    A 2D gameplay experience built around vertical character movement.

    48-Hour Challenge

    Designed and developed within the limited timeframe of Global Game Jam.

    DEVELOPMENT

    Global Game Jam

    The game was developed as part of Global Game Jam at King Abdulaziz University, where our team had 48 hours to design, develop, and present the game.

    Working within the time constraint required the team to rapidly develop the concept and turn it into a playable experience.
