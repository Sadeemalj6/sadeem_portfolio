OVERVIEW

About OreSafe

OreSafe is an interactive safety training platform designed for mining operations, focusing on improving workers' awareness, decision-making, and readiness in hazardous environments.

The concept was developed as part of the 2026 Future Minerals Pioneers challenge with team Decision Mine.

THE PROBLEM

Safer Training for Hazardous Environments

Traditional mining safety training often relies on theoretical lectures or direct field training.

OreSafe addresses this challenge by creating a safer environment where workers can practice responding to realistic situations before facing them in the field.

SOLUTION

What We Built

OreSafe transforms safety training into an interactive and scenario-based experience.

Interactive Training

Realistic mining-site scenarios that allow trainees to practice safety procedures in an immersive environment.

Decision-Making

Trainees make decisions during scenarios, with their choices influencing their training path.

Performance Evaluation

Trainee performance is assessed after each stage to track progress and readiness.

Digital Certification

Successful completion of the training can be followed by a digital certificate.

DEVELOPMENT

Built in One Day

OreSafe was developed and presented within a single-day challenge, from 9:00 AM to 10:00 PM.

Within the challenge period, our team developed the concept, structured the solution, prepared the presentation, and delivered the final pitch.

IMPACT & VISION

Beyond Mining

OreSafe aims to improve workers' awareness and response, reduce workplace accidents, and contribute to a stronger safety culture.

The platform concept can potentially expand beyond mining into industries such as oil, gas, and construction.
