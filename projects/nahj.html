OVERVIEW

About Nahj

Nahj is an intelligent educational solution designed to enhance the digital learning experience for university students in Saudi Arabia.

The project integrates AI-powered educational support into the existing learning environment, providing students with a more personalized and interactive way to engage with their courses.

SOLUTION

What We Built

Nahj combines several intelligent educational capabilities to support students throughout their learning journey.

Intelligent Academic Support

Personalized educational guidance and support based on the student's learning context.

Adaptive Learning

AI-powered learning interactions designed to adapt to the student's needs.

Performance Analysis

Analyzing learning outcomes to provide more relevant educational support.

Intelligent Content Processing

Processing educational content to support intelligent interactions with students.

DEVELOPMENT

How We Worked

The project was developed collaboratively through structured sprints, with the team progressing through each stage of development step by step.

Each sprint focused on a specific part of the project, followed by documentation, evaluation, and reporting before moving to the next stage.
