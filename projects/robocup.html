About the Competition

RoboCup is a robotics competition where each team develops two robots to compete in football matches. The robots are controlled remotely using PlayStation 4 controllers, with each robot programmed to perform different roles on the field.

Our team designed and programmed two robots, configuring their controls and movement according to the roles assigned to each player. Throughout the competition, we won every match without a single loss.
